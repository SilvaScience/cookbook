Dates and potato casserole

# Ingredients
- A hand full of dates with pits removed
- Peeled potatoes cut into roughly 1inch cubes
- onions
- cheese
- black beer or wine (30 ml for 10 inch diameter 1 inch deep skillet)
- Cheddar cheese

# Preparation
- Boil the potatoes until they are roughly cooked
- In a skillet that goes in the oven, cook the onions until they become dark brown
- Add the wine or beer and stir the onions to get the caramelized onion sugar off the bottom of the skillet and into a sauce
- Add the dates and a bit of water and let simmer for a few minutes so that the dates start dissolving
- Mix in the potatoes and add a pinch of salt
- Grade the cheeze on top of the skillet
- Put in the oven on broil until the cheese is melted and browned up a bit

Enjoy!!
