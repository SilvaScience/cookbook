# Carlota de Limon

## Ingredients
- 5 lemon
- 1 condensed (matter) milk can
- 1 cookies pack, but they have to be "Marias" like cookies
- 250gr of cream cheese

## Procedure
1.- Mix the cheese, the condensed (matter) milk, and pour the limon juice in the mix.
2.- In a nice cointainer, better if it is a big glass rectangle, and accomodate the cookies 
at the bottom of it.
3.- Add come of the mixture, enough to cover the cookies.
4.- Add another layer of coockies, imagine it is a cristal and the cookies are the atoms.
5.-Pour more of the cheese-milk mixture, this is kind of a lasagna topology
6.- Refrigerate it
7.- Enjoy!
