# Potato Mustard Dish

## Ingredients
- 2 stripped Potatoes, cut into 1-2inch chunks
- a can of red beans
- Dijon mustard
- A few chopped up leaves of Kale or spinach
- Shredded cheese (Cheddar is ideal but any cheese that melts is good too) 
- Olive oil
- Pepper and salt

## Preparation
1. Boil the potatoes until they are ready and tasty
2. Mix the ingredients together

Enjoy!
